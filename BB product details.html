Knowledge to Policy

An investigative look at whether academic research actually changes the real world.

Key Topics:

Impact: How research influences public policy and government decision-making.

Governance: Using knowledge to hold governments accountable and enlarge policy options.

Case Studies: Real-world examples from Asia, Africa, and Latin America on poverty, trade, and health reform.

Social Science Learning in Schools

A deep dive into how we teach students about society, culture, and their place in the world. Social Themes: Exploring nationalism, identity, and the integrated study of History, Geography, and Civics.

Key Topics:

Pedagogy: How to take social science beyond just "facts" and into critical thinking.

Curriculum Design: Insights into how textbooks are developed and the role of the teacher.

Social Themes: Exploring nationalism, identity, and the integrated study of History, Geography, and Civics.

Understanding Calculus

A student favorite because it skips the "scary" formal proofs and focuses on clear, geometric, and physical examples.

Key Topics:

Foundational concepts: Limits, Derivatives, and Integrals.

Practical Math: The Chain Rule, substitution methods, and curve sketching.

Advanced Basics: Logarithmic and exponential functions.
